## Lo que reportó el agente

Here are a few ways to describe a "clean slate" depending on the tone and context you are aiming for:

### **Idiomatic & Poetic**
*   **Tabula rasa:** (Latin for "blank slate") A neat, academic way to describe a completely fresh start.
*   **A new lease on life:** Suggests revitalization and a second chance at success or happiness.
*   **Turning over a new leaf:** A classic idiom meaning to make a fresh start and adopt better habits.
*   **A blank canvas:** Evokes art and creativity—the idea that you get to paint your future however you want.

### **Modern & Professional**
*   **A fresh start:** Simple, direct, and universally understood.
*   **A reboot / Reset:** Great for professional, tech-inspired, or strategic contexts (e.g., "Let's do a complete project reset").
*   **Starting from scratch:** Implies discarding the old method entirely to build something better from the ground up.
*   **Hitting the reset button:** A dynamic way to describe wiping away past mistakes and beginning again.

### **Short & Punchy**
*   **Zero hour:** Dramatic, often used in military, gaming, or high-stakes contexts.
*   **A clean break:** Best used when you are cutting ties with the past (a bad habit, a job, or a relationship) to move forward.
*   **Wiping the slate clean:** Focuses specifically on forgiving past mistakes or erasing previous debts/errors.

**Example in a sentence:** 
> *"After the failed merger, the company needed a **clean slate** to rebuild its reputation with investors."*
